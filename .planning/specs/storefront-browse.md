# Spec: Feature 10 — Storefront Browse (Epic E2)

**Created**: 2026-07-28 · **Status**: draft — awaiting Gate 1 · **Epic**: E2 Feature 10 (last E2 feature) · **Effort**: M
**Depends on**: E1 #4 (host→tenant middleware), E2 #7 (profile, hours, exceptions), E2 #8 (dress/variant/media shape + the storage port), E2 #9 (`packages/ui`, tokens, the three gate-passed storefront screen docs) — branches off `main` · **Feeds**: E3 #14 (the booking flow replaces the CTA seam), E5 #29 (slug/config caching), E10 (SEO/prerendering, thumbnails/CDN)

## Problem

Everything the storefront needs exists except the storefront. F7 configured the business, F8 defined the dress/variant/media shape, F9 shipped `packages/ui` with every storefront composite already built and gate-passed — `BoutiqueHeader`, `DressCard`, `DressGrid`, `Gallery`, `Price`, `HoursTable`, `ContactPanel`, `BookingCTA`, `A11yMenu`, `SkipLink`, plus `lib/hours.ts` and `lib/url.ts`. What does not exist is anything that feeds them. `Frontend/apps/storefront/src/App.tsx` is a 13-line placeholder rendering `t("placeholder.heading")`; `Frontend/apps/storefront/vite.config.ts` is four lines with no `server` block, so the app cannot even be opened at `http://{slug}.localtest.me:5173`; and **there is no public read surface in the backend** — 24 of the 26 `/manage` operations carry `Depends(get_current_staff)`, and the two that do not, `POST /manage/auth/login` and `POST /manage/auth/logout`, are both writes. (`logout` reads the `boutique_session` cookie directly and tolerates its absence, so it takes no `Staff` dependency either.)

So F10 is not a UI feature. It is the platform's **first unauthenticated, tenant-scoped, publicly reachable surface**, and the largest part of it is the read API that surface consumes: three GETs that must derive the tenant from DNS and nothing else, and refuse — server-side, by construction — to ship the four categories of manage-only data F8 explicitly fenced off (hidden prices, raw stock counts, derived out-of-stock, archived rows). E2's third success criterion ("customers browse the catalog and dress pages on the tenant subdomain in Hebrew RTL … price visibility respected") closes here or not at all.

## Goal

A bride taps an Instagram-bio link at 23:00 on her phone. `{slug}.{domain}` paints the boutique's name in Frank Ruhl Libre, one essence line, today's hours ("סגור היום · נפתח מחר ב-10:00" if closed — in ink, not danger), a tappable address, and a 2-column grid of 3:4 dress photos with prices or "מחיר בתיאום" occupying the same slot at the same height. Below the grid, "עוד שמלות" brings the next 24. She taps a card, lands on `/dress/{id}`, swipes the gallery, reads the description, sees the size chips and the "הוזמן" badge on a reserved dress whose booking CTA is still live. The CTA opens a contact panel with tap-to-call and WhatsApp — the E3 seam, visually identical to what will replace it. From the footer she reaches `/about` for the story, the full Israeli week of hours with upcoming exceptions, and Waze; and `/accessibility` for the הצהרת נגישות the law requires. Nothing she can see, and nothing in any response body she could open DevTools on, discloses a hidden price, a stock count, an archived dress, or another boutique's anything.

## Design

### Data

**No migration.** F10 adds no table, no column, no index. Every read is served by indexes F7 and F8 already built (`idx_dresses_tenant_active`, `idx_dress_media_dress_ready`, `idx_dress_variants_dress_active`), and `0005_boutique_settings.py:118` / `0006_catalog.py:134` already `GRANT SELECT` on every table F10 touches. `test_every_tenant_id_table_has_forced_rls` therefore has nothing new to pick up, and its continued green is the assertion that F10 did not sneak a table in.

Two non-schema data changes are required, both one-liners:

- **`TenantContext` gains `name: str = ""`** (`app/tenancy/middleware.py:25`). Every storefront screen leads with the boutique name; `tenants.name` is read today only by `app/platform/service.py:115` (the CLI). `RepositoryTenantResolver.__call__` (`app/tenancy/resolver.py:18`) already `SELECT`s the whole row, so this costs **zero extra queries**. **The default is deliberate and is the whole point**: there are nine hand-built `TenantContext`s across five test files (`test_middleware.py:24`, `test_auth_api.py:12`, `test_catalog_api.py:61`, `test_boutique_api.py:32`, `test_catalog_isolation.py:376,377,423,468,469` — note there is no `test_boutique_isolation.py`), and a required field would turn a one-line change into a red push across all five. `resolver.py` is the only production construction site and always populates it; F10's own test fixtures set it explicitly.
- **`AvailabilityExceptionsRepository.list_active` gains `on_or_after: date | None = None`** (`app/db/repositories/availability.py:60`). Today it returns every exception ever recorded, ordered by `date`; `/about` wants upcoming ones only. Defaulting to `None` leaves F7's manage caller byte-identical.

### The F7 profile amendment (delivered here, deliberately)

`_PROFILE_FIELDS` is `frozenset({"phone", "address", "description", "maps_url"})` (`app/boutique/validation.py:48`) and `validate_profile` rejects unknown keys with a 400. Two gate-passed props therefore have no data source: `BoutiqueHeader.essence` and `ContactPanel.instagram`. `qa-checklist.md` §6 makes the first binding — *"`BoutiqueHeader` default carries all four parts — name, essence, hours-today snippet, and the location link"* — so shipping a three-part header is a checklist failure, not a graceful degradation. F10 adds both fields:

- `essence: str | None`, `MAX_PROFILE_ESSENCE_LENGTH = 120` — one line under a `--text-3xl` display heading at 375px.
- `instagram: str | None`, `MAX_PROFILE_INSTAGRAM_LENGTH = 30` (Instagram's own handle ceiling), validated against `^[A-Za-z0-9._]{1,30}$` and stored **without** the `@`, because `ContactPanel` builds `https://instagram.com/{handle}`. A handle that fails the pattern is a `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, not a stored dead link.

**And one line of Hebrew, which is the point of shipping the amendment here.** F7 collected `phone`, `address`, `description` and `maps_url` behind owner auth; F10 is the PR that makes them world-readable, on a page with no consent step and no per-field visibility control (unlike `price_visible`). A home-based boutique's owner typed her home address into a field that has only ever been private. `ProfileSection.tsx` gains a helper line under the section heading — "השדות האלה מופיעים בדף הפומבי של הבוטיק" — and the same statement joins the pilot-onboarding script (Risks 7). Zero backend change; it belongs in this PR because this is the PR that makes it true.

Touched: `app/boutique/validation.py` (two constants, `_PROFILE_FIELDS`, a `validate_instagram_handle`), `app/boutique/schemas.py` `ProfileUpdate` (two `Field`s), `Frontend/apps/manage/src/components/ProfileSection.tsx` + its test, and `manage-restyle.md`'s profile-section row. This is an F7 amendment shipped by its consumer, and it is recorded as such so a reviewer does not read it as scope creep.

**`whatsapp` and `wazeUrl` are derived, not stored.** `validate_phone` allows at most one leading `+` then the charset `0123456789 ()-`, so `waPhone("052-1234567") → "972521234567"` is a pure function: strip non-digits; leading `0` → `972`; already `972` → keep; **anything else → `undefined`** (never guess a country code — a wrong `wa.me` link is worse than no link). `wazeUrl` is `https://waze.com/ul?q={encodeURIComponent(address)}`. Both live in `apps/storefront/src/lib/contact.ts` and are unit-tested; neither adds a column.

### What the storefront must never see (the field allowlist, stated once)

This is the security core of the feature, and it is enforced by **narrow response models plus an assertion per field**, not by remembering to omit things.

| Field | Where it lives | Public treatment |
|---|---|---|
| `price_agorot` with `price_visible = false` | `dresses` | **`null`.** The number never leaves the process. `price_visible` itself is **not serialised at all** |
| `price_agorot IS NULL` with `price_visible = true` | `dresses` | `null` — same wire value, same "מחיר בתיאום" render. Collapsing the two cases is deliberate: a client that could tell them apart could tell that *this* dress has a hidden price |
| `dress_variants.quantity` | F8's Note for F10 | **never sent.** Public variant shape is `{size_label, available: quantity > 0}` |
| `out_of_stock`, `total_quantity`, `variant_count` | derived in `CatalogService._stock_summary` | **never computed.** The public list does not call `DressVariantsRepository.aggregate_by_dress` at all — one statement saved per page and one leak made unreachable |
| `archived` / `deleted_at IS NOT NULL` | `dresses` | never returned, never listed, and a direct hit is `404 NOT_FOUND` indistinguishable from a missing id |
| `media_count`, `media_uploads_enabled`, `media_slots_remaining`, media `id`/`content_type`/`byte_size` | F8's `DressDetailResponse` | never sent as fields — gallery slot accounting is an owner concern |
| `sort_order` (dress, variant, media) | all three tables | never sent; it is expressed as list order |
| `created_at` / `updated_at` | `StandardColumns` | never sent |
| `availability_rules.capacity`, and every row `id` | `availability_rules` / `availability_exceptions` | never sent. `capacity` is booking-engine internal and discloses how many parallel fittings the boutique runs |
| `tenants.settings.toggles` (`deposits_enabled`, `brides_only`) | settings JSONB | not sent — browse-only F10 renders no appointment type. `brides_only`'s storefront semantics land with E3 #14 |
| `tenants.id`, `tenants.slug`, `tenants.status` | `tenants` | not sent **as fields**. `tenants.id`, `dresses.id` and `dress_media.id` **are** disclosed inside every signed media URL — `build_media_key` is `tenants/{tenant_id}/dresses/{dress_id}/media/{media_id}.jpg`, presigned against `https://s3.{region}.amazonaws.com/{bucket}/…`, so the bucket name and region are public too. That is inherent to direct-to-S3 with the tenant-prefixed keys `security-checklist-v1.md:11` requires, and it is not a leak of anything the visitor's own hostname does not already imply |

`test_storefront_api.py` asserts the **absence of each key** against a fully-populated fake, not merely the presence of the ones we want. It is a key-absence assertion by design: a substring assertion such as `str(tenant_id) not in response.text` would fail on the media URL above and would then get "fixed" by weakening the test rather than by correcting the claim.

### Named constants (`app/storefront/validation.py`)

Nothing here is mirrored to the frontend — no F10 constant is enforced client-side, so `test_frontend_constant_parity.py` gains no rows. Deliberately not env-tunable, per F8's rule that `Settings` carries deployment identity and never product policy.

| Constant | Value | Why this number |
|---|---|---|
| `STOREFRONT_LIST_DEFAULT_LIMIT` | 24 | same as `DRESS_LIST_DEFAULT_LIMIT`; the grid is 2/3/4-col, so 24 fills 6/8/12 rows and the worst case is 24 unprocessed covers |
| `STOREFRONT_LIST_MAX_LIMIT` | 24 | **equal to the default, unlike manage's 100.** The UI never asks for more, and every extra unit is a 4× denial-of-wallet multiplier: one unauthenticated call mints one fresh 900 s redeemable URL per item, worth up to the 10 MiB upload cap, redeemable by any third party (Risks 3). Symmetry with the authenticated manage list is not a reason. Raise it in the feature that ships a UI which asks for more |
| `UPCOMING_EXCEPTIONS_LIMIT` | 12 | one editorial card's worth. A boutique that records every holiday for three years must not turn `/about` into a calendar |
| `MAX_PROFILE_ESSENCE_LENGTH` | 120 | one line under `--text-3xl` at 375px |
| `MAX_PROFILE_INSTAGRAM_LENGTH` | 30 | Instagram's handle ceiling |

Read-throttle bounds are **`Settings` fields**, following the `media_presign_*` and `login_max_attempts` precedent so they can be tightened during an incident without a deploy: `storefront_read_max_per_window: int = 6000`, `storefront_read_window_seconds: int = 60`. **The arithmetic, because the analogy to `login_max_attempts` is wrong here**: a first paint is 2 requests (`getBoutique` at layout mount + the route's own call), a dress tap is 1 more. 6000/60 s is ~3000 first-paints per minute per tenant. The obvious number, 600, would 429 real customers the minute a boutique's Instagram story lands — the exact traffic event the product exists for — while a scraper simply paces itself. This is a runaway brake, sized so it cannot fire on organic traffic.

### API (`app/storefront/` module: `router.py`, `schemas.py`, `service.py`, `validation.py`)

`APIRouter(prefix="/storefront", dependencies=[Depends(_no_store), Depends(_throttle)])`, registered in `create_app()` after the catalog router. The prefix is **outside `/manage`** for three reasons, all load-bearing: `CsrfOriginMiddleware.PROTECTED_PREFIX` is `"/manage"` and F10 has no mutating route to protect; `app/boutique/router.py` and `app/catalog/router.py` both mount `/manage`, so a third router there would make path shadowing a three-way hazard (`test_catalog_api.py:103`'s `ROUTES` table exists because of exactly this); and the dev proxy needs one unambiguous prefix to forward.

**Auth posture, stated as an invariant.** No route takes `Staff`. `Depends(get_current_staff)` is the codebase's one and only owner-only marker, and its *absence* is what makes a route public — there is no role guard, scope or annotation to also remove. The precedent is `POST /manage/auth/login` (`app/auth/router.py:42`) — one of the two existing routes that take no `Staff`, alongside `POST /manage/auth/logout` — which is already tenant-scoped and resolves its tenant from the Host exactly as F10 will. **Storefront paths must never be added to `EXEMPT_PATHS`** (`app/tenancy/middleware.py:16`) — that frozenset skips tenant resolution entirely, and a storefront route that reached a handler without `request.state.tenant` would raise `TenantNotResolvedError`. The set is exact-match, not prefix, so this cannot happen by accident; the test asserts it anyway.

**The OpenAPI schema and docs stop being public, in this PR.** `EXEMPT_PATHS` also carries `/openapi.json`, `/docs`, `/docs/oauth2-redirect` and `/redoc`, exempted from tenant resolution with the comment *"the OpenAPI schema feeds api-client generation … revisited at the Feature 21 hardening gate"*, and `create_app()` passes no `docs_url`/`openapi_url`. F10 makes the origin a crawled public surface, and the first crawler that finds `{slug}.{domain}` also finds `/openapi.json` — a complete, uncredentialed description of every `/manage` route and of exactly the fields the allowlist above exists to fence off (`quantity`, `price_visible`, `out_of_stock`, `capacity`, `terms_text`, the presign shape). F21 is the gate *after* the pilot is already public. F10 therefore passes `docs_url=redoc_url=openapi_url=None` unless `settings.app_env == "dev"`, and amends the `EXEMPT_PATHS` comment: the api-client justification is void anyway, because F10 declines the generated client (below). `test_openapi_is_unreachable_outside_dev` asserts it.

**Tenant binding, and exactly how far RLS reaches.** `tenant = get_current_tenant(request)` → `tenant.id` → `tenant_session(session_factory, tenant.id)`, which runs `SELECT set_config('app.tenant_id', :value, true)` transaction-locally. This is the *same* two-step every manage route uses, and nothing about it depends on authentication: `TenantResolutionMiddleware` runs on every non-exempt path regardless of session, `extract_slug` rejects non-ASCII hosts, IPv6 literals and anything not ending in `.{base_domain}`, `is_valid_slug` rejects the reserved set, and `TenantsRepository.by_slug` filters `status == ACTIVE AND deleted_at IS NULL`. **Tenant identity derives from DNS, never from auth and never from client input.**

Be precise about the guarantee, because the two halves are not the same: **dresses, variants, media and availability are FORCE-RLS protected** via `tenant_session`, with the repositories' redundant explicit `tenant_id` predicate on top. **`tenants` is not** — `app/models/tenant.py:12` says so ("this IS the tenant registry"), and `TenantsRepository.by_slug` opens a plain session with no `app.tenant_id` set. So `GET /storefront/boutique` — name, essence, description, phone, address, maps_url, instagram, the whole public identity block — is protected by host-derived slug lookup **and nothing else**. That is the entire guarantee, and it matters because E5 #29 puts a cache in front of exactly that lookup; a cache keyed on anything but the exact slug, or a `TenantContext` that outlives its request, publishes boutique A's phone and home address on boutique B's domain. The isolation suite therefore carries a named assertion for it (Testing), not only the cross-tenant dress-id probe.

**Cookies are not read and must not be sent.** No storefront handler touches `boutique_session`. The storefront and the manage console share the origin `{slug}.{domain}` (console at `/manage`), so the browser *would* attach the session cookie if the client asked it to — `apps/storefront/src/api.ts` uses `credentials: "omit"`, and a test asserts that a request carrying a valid owner cookie gets a byte-identical response to one carrying none. A public endpoint that behaves differently for a logged-in owner is a public endpoint with a hidden second contract.

**`Cache-Control: no-store` on the router, not per route.** Two of the three responses carry signed GET URLs valid for `SIGNED_GET_TTL_SECONDS = 900`; the F8 rationale (`app/catalog/router.py:44`) applies verbatim and is reproduced in the docstring. Uniform application means a route added later cannot forget it. The cost is real and is recorded in Risks 3: the public catalog is uncacheable at every layer.

| Method + path | Request | Response | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GET /storefront/boutique` | — | `BoutiqueResponse` | 2 statements. Name, profile and toggles come free from `request.state.tenant` — the resolver already fetched the row |
| `GET /storefront/dresses` | query `offset`, `limit` | `DressListResponse` | 3 statements. `archived` and `search` are **not accepted** — no query param on this route can reach a repository predicate other than paging |
| `GET /storefront/dresses/{dress_id}` | — | `DressDetailResponse` | 3 statements. Archived or unknown ⇒ `404 NOT_FOUND` |

```
BoutiqueResponse   { name, essence: str|null, description: str|null, phone: str|null,

HoursRow           { day_of_week: int, open_time: time, close_time: time }   # one row PER WINDOW
ExceptionRow       { date, open_time: time|null, close_time: time|null, note: str|null }

StorefrontDetail   { id, name, description: str|null, price_agorot: int|null, reserved: bool,

SizeChip           { size_label: str, available: bool }
StorefrontMedia    { url: str|null, url_expires_at: datetime|null }
DressListResponse  { items: [StorefrontDress], total, offset, limit }
```

`items` is the house envelope key (F8 declared it). `hours` is **flat rows, one per window** — F7 allows multiple windows per day (`app/models/availability.py:13`) and the repository orders by `day_of_week, open_time`; the wire shape stays a straight projection and the client groups (Frontend, below). Router paging signature copies F8's spelled-out form: `offset: Annotated[int, Query(ge=0)] = 0`, `limit: Annotated[int, Query(ge=1, le=STOREFRONT_LIST_MAX_LIMIT)] = STOREFRONT_LIST_DEFAULT_LIMIT`. The service clamps both again so a non-router caller cannot request an unbounded page.

**Repository reuse — every read path is an existing method called with existing arguments.**

- `DressesRepository.list_page(session, tenant_id, archived=False, search=None, offset=…, limit=…)` — ordered `sort_order, created_at DESC, id`, fully covered by `idx_dresses_tenant_active`. Verbatim.
- `DressesRepository.count(session, tenant_id, archived=False, search=None)` — verbatim. Its result is `DressListResponse.total`, and it is **consumed**: it is what the load-more button counts against.
- `DressesRepository.by_id(session, tenant_id, dress_id)` — pins `deleted_at IS NULL`, so an archived dress is an indistinguishable 404, which is exactly the design's "השמלה כבר לא זמינה" state. Verbatim. **`by_id_any_state` must never be reachable from `app/storefront/`** — a test greps the module for the symbol.
- `DressMediaRepository.covers_by_dress(session, tenant_id, dress_ids)` — `DISTINCT ON` + window count, one statement, pins `status = READY AND deleted_at IS NULL`. Verbatim; the window `media_count` is discarded.
- `DressMediaRepository.list_ready(session, tenant_id, dress_id)` — verbatim.
- `DressVariantsRepository.list_active(session, tenant_id, dress_id)` — verbatim; `quantity` is folded to `available = quantity > 0` in the projection and never survives the frame.
- `AvailabilityRulesRepository.list_active(session, tenant_id)` — ordered `day_of_week, open_time`. Verbatim.
- `AvailabilityExceptionsRepository.list_active(session, tenant_id, on_or_after=today_jerusalem())` — the one signature change.

`aggregate_by_dress` and `count_active` are **not called**. That is a deliberate two-statement saving per page *and* the structural reason `out_of_stock` cannot leak.

**`today_jerusalem()`** is `datetime.datetime.now(ZoneInfo("Asia/Jerusalem")).date()`, in `app/storefront/validation.py`, with an injectable `clock` on `StorefrontService` for the test. `qa-checklist.md` §3 binds "today" to IANA `Asia/Jerusalem` on the frontend; the backend cutoff must agree or an exception dated today disappears from `/about` for the hours the two calendars differ.

**Signed-URL signing is shared, not forked.** `CatalogService._media_view` (`app/catalog/service.py:708`) contains the invariant that a signing failure — a rotated credential while `is_configured` still returns `True` — degrades to `url = None` with a WARNING rather than 503-ing the whole read. That behaviour must not exist in two places. It is hoisted verbatim to a module-level `sign_media(storage: MediaStorage, row: DressMedia) -> MediaView` in `app/catalog/service.py`; `_media_view` becomes a one-line delegate (no behaviour change, F8's tests untouched) and `StorefrontService` imports it. Signing is local HMAC, so it is called **outside** `tenant_session` — F8's transaction-boundaries invariant applies unchanged, and F10 makes no storage network call at all.

**Read throttle.** `_throttle` is a router-level dependency: `key = f"storefront:{tenant.id}"`; `if limiter.is_blocked(key): raise StorefrontThrottledError`; otherwise `limiter.record_failure(key)` immediately (the limiter counts only what is explicitly recorded — the `create_terms_version` and F8-presign precedent) and serve. A fourth `FixedWindowRateLimiter` is built in `create_app()` from the two new settings with `clock=time.monotonic`. **This does mean a new exception class and a new handler** — there is no shared throttle base: `RateLimitedError`, `TermsThrottledError` and `MediaPresignThrottledError` are three unrelated plain-`Exception` subclasses with three handlers, and importing a *media presign* error into `app/storefront/` to dodge a fourth would be a semantic lie no test catches. `StorefrontThrottledError` + one three-line handler returning the existing `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS_BODY` is the smaller, honest diff; reparenting all four onto a `DomainThrottledError` base is a behaviour-neutral cleanup worth doing in F21, not cross-module churn in this PR. **Be honest about what the throttle is**: it brakes a scraper's rate and bounds one tenant's DB read amplification. It is in-process and single-instance (`app/auth/rate_limit.py:6-15`), keyed per *tenant* not per IP, so a determined scraper degrades the boutique it is scraping, and it does not bound S3 egress at all — a minted URL is redeemable by anyone for 900 s. Per-IP keying needs `trust_forwarded_for`, which the Cloudflare-fallback two-hop caveat (`.planning/plans/staging-and-external-apps.md:22`) leaves unresolved. Owner: F21. Recorded, not oversold.

**Security headers (new middleware, `app/security_headers.py`).** `grep -iE "x-frame-options|content-security|strict-transport|x-content-type|referrer-policy"` over `Backend/app` returns nothing today. `.planning/security-checklist-v1.md:33` is F21's line, but F10 is what makes the origin publicly reachable. **Registered last in `create_app()`, after `CsrfOriginMiddleware`, so it is outermost** — otherwise it misses the single most-served public response, the `404 TENANT_NOT_FOUND` that `TenantResolutionMiddleware.dispatch` returns itself (`middleware.py:54,57`) without ever reaching a handler. Three headers on every response:

- `X-Frame-Options: DENY` — platform-wide. **Rationale stated correctly**: this is API defense-in-depth on JSON responses. It is *not* the clickjacking fix for the manage console, because the framable document is `index.html`, served by Vite in dev and by an as-yet-nonexistent static host in production — neither sees this middleware. That requirement is added to Risks 2 and owned by the frontend-deploy follow-up. Embedding a tenant storefront in a third-party site is not supported in v1; a per-tenant `frame-ancestors` allowlist is E10's if a boutique asks.
- `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` — genuinely load-bearing on JSON.
- `Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin`

**HSTS and CSP are explicitly deferred** with reasons, not silence: HSTS needs the real domain and a TLS-termination decision that `external-applications.md` #2 blocks, and shipping `max-age` against a `.invalid` staging host is meaningless; a CSP for a Vite bundle needs a nonce or hash story authored against a deployed artifact, and there is no frontend deploy pipeline to author it against. Owner: F21. Trigger: domain purchased.

**No CORS. Ever.** `Frontend/apps/manage/vite.config.ts` states the rule and F10 inherits it: same-origin in production, Vite proxy in dev. There is no `CORSMiddleware` in the codebase and F10 does not add one. Note for the reviewer who reaches for it: **bucket CORS is irrelevant to F10's read path.** `DressCard` and `Gallery` render plain `<img src>` with no `crossorigin` attribute, and a no-CORS image subresource load is not preflighted and not subject to `AllowedOrigins`. Bucket CORS governs F8's *upload* POST only. (This does not rescue staging — see Risks 1 — but it removes it as an F10 blocker.)

Errors — all in the house shape `{"error": {"code", "message"}}`. F10 registers **one** new exception handler (`StorefrontThrottledError` → 429); `CatalogNotFoundError` already subclasses `DomainNotFoundError`, whose handler is bound to the base class (`app/main.py:226`), and the 400 handler already exists.

| Code | Status | Raised when |
|---|---|---|
| `TENANT_NOT_FOUND` | 404 | unknown / suspended / soft-deleted / reserved slug, apex, foreign or missing Host — `TenantResolutionMiddleware`, before any handler |
| `NOT_FOUND` | 404 | unknown or **archived** `dress_id`, and another tenant's `dress_id` — one indistinguishable answer, by design |
| `VALIDATION_ERROR` | 400 | `offset < 0`, `limit < 1`, `limit > 24`, a non-UUID `dress_id` |
| `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS` | 429 | the per-tenant read throttle (existing body, new handler) |

No 401 (nothing to authenticate), no 403 (no CSRF surface — every route is `GET`), no 503 (a missing bucket serialises `url: null`; only F8's *write* endpoints 503).

### Frontend (`Frontend/apps/storefront`)

**`packages/ui` gains exactly two optional props and nothing else.** Every composite the three screen docs name is already exported from `Frontend/packages/ui/src/index.ts` and gate-passed, and the four gaps the design leaves (the exceptions row under `HoursTable`, the Hebrew hours sentence, the description clamp, the share button) are all app-level composition. **Routing — hand-rolled, no new runtime dependency.** No router exists anywhere in the monorepo (`react-router`, `wouter`, `@tanstack/router`: zero hits across all four `package.json`s). `apps/storefront/src/router.tsx` is ~80 lines: `useSyncExternalStore` over `popstate`, a four-pattern matcher (`/`, `/dress/:id`, `/about`, `/accessibility`), and **one delegated click listener at the app root**. Delegation is what makes this work without touching `packages/ui`: `DressCard` renders a raw `<a href>` and takes no `onNavigate`, `A11yStatementLink` is a plain `<a>`, and the back-link is a plain `<a href="/">`.

The predicate is *not* "same-origin left-click without modifiers" — stated that loosely it swallows `SkipLink`, which is a plain `<a href="#content">` (`A11y.tsx:16`), so `preventDefault()` + `pushState` would fire, the browser would never perform the fragment navigation, focus would never move, and the WCAG item the e2e suite asserts would fail. **The exclusion list is part of the contract**: skip interception when the href is hash-only or resolves to the current `pathname` with a `hash`; when `target` is set; when `download` is set; when `rel` contains `external`; and when the resolved protocol is not `http:`/`https:` (`ContactPanel.tsx:37` emits `tel:`, and its four external links carry `target="_blank"`). `router.test.tsx` asserts a `#content` click is **not** intercepted.

**`navigate(path)` moves focus and resets scroll.** A `pushState` that unmounts the focused `<a>` drops `document.activeElement` to `<body>`: a screen-reader user who activates a `DressCard` hears nothing at all and the next Tab restarts from the top, and a sighted user lands on `/dress/{id}` still scrolled to grid row 5. Both are WCAG 2.0 Level A (2.4.3) and are the canonical hand-rolled-SPA failure. After the new route commits, `navigate` calls `window.scrollTo(0, 0)` and focuses the `<main id="content" tabIndex={-1}>` that `StorefrontLayout` already creates for the skip link — which is also what announces the new page, so no extra live region. `history.back()` / `navigate(-1)` / `router.back()` appear nowhere; `qa-checklist.md` §11 greps for them, and the reason is that the primary entry is an Instagram deep link with no history stack.

The single-page-app fallback: Vite's default `appType: "spa"` makes both `vite dev` and `vite preview` serve `index.html` for unknown paths. **The plan must verify this against `vite preview --port 4173 --strictPort` before relying on it** — the blocking `e2e` job navigates directly to `/dress/{id}` and `/about`, and if the fallback does not hold, the config change belongs in the same commit.

**API client — hand-written, and `@boutique/api-client` stays empty.** `packages/api-client/src/index.ts` is `export {}` with a comment naming F10 as the feature that fills it. **UI behaviour.**

*Data fetching, and how the two calls compose.* Plain `useEffect` + a `let cancelled = false` guard + cleanup, mirroring `ProfileSection.tsx:44-61`. No react-query, no SWR, no AbortController, no new dependency. **`getBoutique()` is issued once, by `StorefrontLayout`, and consumed by all four routes** — the footer's phone/Instagram, the CTA's `ContactPanel`, the header and `/accessibility`'s complaints contact all need it, so a per-route fetch would leave `/dress/{id}`'s footer and its CTA panel empty. Each route then issues at most its own data call. `/accessibility` issues none.

`/` therefore has two independent in-flight fetches and four states, all specified: **boutique pending** → `BoutiqueHeader` region renders `Skeleton`, grid skeletons render independently (§6's "header + 6 skeleton cards"); **dresses pending, boutique resolved** → real header, six skeleton cards; **dresses failed** → the inline retry block *under a real header*, identity intact, `<p role="alert" className="text-base text-ink-muted">` — muted, not danger — with a secondary `Button` retry; **boutique failed** → identity is what failed, so "identity intact" is unachievable: degrade to `/about`'s error pattern (§6) — the message plus whatever contact affordance is available — and the CTA button is not rendered rather than opening an empty `ContactPanel`. A read `429` renders the same retry state, never a blank page.

*Load-more, because 24 is not the catalog.* `catalog-management.md:375` sizes the pilot at "all ~60 dresses", so a single un-paged call leaves 36 unreachable and E2's third success criterion fails. `total` is already on the wire and is consumed: while `items.length < total`, a `Button variant="secondary"` appends the next page at `offset += STOREFRONT_LIST_DEFAULT_LIMIT`. That is the whole feature — no infinite scroll, no scroll restoration, no count label. The *filter* half of the deferred bundle (`הצגת 24 מתוך 57` + 3 facets) stays post-pilot; the load-more is not an enhancement, it is the only path to dress 25.

*Hours: the wire shape is not `lib/hours.ts`'s shape, and the gap is where the bugs are.* `hoursText.ts` owns both transforms and both are unit-tested:
- **Flat rows → `WeeklyRule[]`.** `hours` carries one row per window, and `groupWeeklyRules` does `byDay.set(rule.dayOfWeek, …)`, so a naive 1:1 map keeps only the last window and a boutique with a lunch break renders half its hours. Group by `day_of_week` into `windows: [{open, close}]`, slicing `HH:MM:SS` → `HH:MM` exactly as `HoursSection.tsx:12` does.
- **`ExceptionRow[]` → `Exceptions`.** `todayHours()` and `nextOpen()` both accept `exceptions?: Record<"YYYY-MM-DD", TimeWindow[]>` and **both `/` and `/about` must pass it** — otherwise a boutique closed for a holiday advertises that it is open, on exactly the days that matter. `open_time == null ⇒ []` (closed all day, per `app/models/availability.py:26`), else `[{open, close}]`.

Neither `nextOpen` result is guaranteed: a boutique with **no `availability_rules` at all** is the state every new tenant ships in (E2 criterion 1 is still unchecked), and `nextOpen` returns `null`. `about.hoursUnavailable` is a real Hebrew string for that case; `BoutiqueHeader.hoursText` is **omitted entirely** on `/` (the prop is optional and a header line saying "hours unknown" is worse than no line), while `/about`'s card renders the string above the all-closed `HoursTable`. *Signed-URL expiry.* URLs live 900 s; a bride who leaves the tab open past that sees broken images. ## Edge cases

1. **Unknown, suspended, soft-deleted or reserved slug, apex, or a foreign Host** → one byte-identical `404 TENANT_NOT_FOUND` before any handler runs, carrying the three security headers. A public storefront that returns 200 with content is itself an existence oracle — that is inherent to DNS and is not what this defends; what it defends is *distinguishing* unknown from suspended.
2. **Archived dress requested directly** → `by_id` pins `deleted_at IS NULL` ⇒ `404 NOT_FOUND`, identical to a never-existing id. The page renders "השמלה כבר לא זמינה" plus a back-to-catalog CTA — a storefront 404-within-tenant, not a platform 404.
3. **Cross-tenant probe with no cookie** → `404 NOT_FOUND`. RLS (`FORCE`, `USING tenant_id = current_setting('app.tenant_id', true)::uuid`) plus the repositories' redundant explicit `tenant_id` predicate make it indistinguishable from missing. **This is the single most important assertion F10 adds**, because it is the first time the guarantee is exercised with no session in play. Its sibling — `GET /storefront/boutique` on host A never returning any of B's profile — is asserted separately, because that path has no RLS to lean on.
4. **`price_visible = false`** → `price_agorot: null` on the wire and `price_visible` absent entirely; the grid renders "מחיר בתיאום" in the same slot at the same height as a priced sibling, so a mixed grid has no layout jump.
5. **`price_agorot IS NULL` with `price_visible = true`** → identical wire value and identical render. Valid state; visibility is a permission, not a promise that a price exists (F8 edge case 14).
6. **Reserved dress** → `Badge variant="muted"` "הוזמן" at `top-3 start-3`, card **not dimmed**, CTA on the detail page **still live**.
7. **Out-of-stock dress** → renders exactly like any other dress. `out_of_stock` is manage-only and F10 never computes it; there is no storefront badge for it.
8. **Dress with zero photos** → `cover: null` ⇒ `DressCard` emits **no `<img>` at all** and fills the 3:4 slot with its own monogram; in a mixed row the photo-less card and its photographed sibling have identical height. The detail `Gallery` returns `null` for 0 images and the page renders `Monogram` (the boutique initial) instead. Two distinct components, both checked.
9. **Bucket unconfigured, or credentials rotated mid-flight** → `sign_media` returns `url: None` with a WARNING; every card and the whole gallery degrade to the monogram. No 503 on any read path.
10. **Signed URL expires during a long session** → the first image `onError` (via the new `onImageError` prop) triggers exactly one refetch of that page's data (`hasRefreshedRef`); a still-failing image falls back to the monogram rather than retrying.
11. **Boutique with zero dresses** (the state the storefront ships in, before the pilot uploads anything) → the **critical** empty state: name, essence, today's hours, address link, an inline `ContactPanel`, the CTA bar, and "הקולקציה בדרך" in `--color-ink-muted`. No load-more button. The storefront must feel finished with an empty catalog.
12. **More than 24 dresses** (the pilot ships with ~60) → the first page is 24 and "עוד שמלות" appends the next 24 until `items.length === total`, then the button disappears. A 25th dress reachable from the UI is a Vitest assertion, not an assumption.
13. **A boutique with no `availability_rules` rows at all** → `nextOpen` returns `null`; `/` omits the header hours line entirely, `/about` renders `about.hoursUnavailable` above an all-`סגור` week. Never blank, never `undefined`.
14. **Saturday, or any single day with no rule** → `HoursTable` renders the `שבת` row with the literal "סגור" — never blank, never `—`, never an omitted row. A fixture that omits Saturday entirely must still find both strings in the DOM.
15. **Two windows on one day** (F7 allows a lunch break) → both windows render in the same row. A naive one-row-per-wire-row map silently keeps the last; the grouping fixture asserts both.
16. **An exception on today's date** → `open_time IS NULL` ⇒ closed all day, so `/` and `/about` both say "סגור היום" on a holiday even though the weekly rule says open; both times set ⇒ the special hours render. Passing `exceptions` into `todayHours`/`nextOpen` is what makes this true, and it is asserted on both branches.
17. **Non-adjacent identical hours** — Sun/Mon/Wed/Thu 10:00–19:00, **Tue no rule**, Fri 09:00–13:00, Sat none → five rows (`א׳–ב׳` / `ג׳ סגור` / `ד׳–ה׳` / `ו׳` / `שבת סגור`) and **never** `א׳–ה׳`. `groupWeeklyRules` already does this; the assertion is that F10 feeds it the whole week, not only the days that have rows.
18. **23:30 Asia/Jerusalem** → "today" flips at Jerusalem midnight on both sides of the wire: `nextOpen`/`todayHours` anchor day stepping at noon UTC so DST cannot shift the calendar day, and the backend's `on_or_after` cutoff uses `ZoneInfo("Asia/Jerusalem")`. A frozen-clock test asserts an identical render under `TZ=America/New_York` and `TZ=Asia/Jerusalem`. `נפתח מחר ב-…` is emitted **only** when the next open window is literally the next calendar day.
19. **Non-JSON 200 from the API** — the exact state the blocking `e2e` job runs in → `apiFetch` raises `ApiError`, the page renders its error state with identity intact, and nothing throws an unhandled `SyntaxError`.
20. **A visitor arriving with a valid owner session cookie** (the owner previewing her own storefront) → byte-identical response. `credentials: "omit"` client-side, no handler reads a cookie server-side.
21. **`limit=24`** → 200; **`limit=25`** → `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`. **`?archived=true` or `?search=x`** → the params do not exist on the route and are silently ignored by FastAPI; no repository predicate can be reached from a query string other than paging.
22. **Read throttle trips** → `429 TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS`, which the client renders as the retry error state (not a blank page). Keyed per tenant, so the storefront degrades for everyone during a scrape — recorded honestly in Risks 4.
23. **A skip-link click, a `tel:` link, a `target="_blank"` link** → not intercepted by the router's click delegation; the browser handles each natively.

## Risks & open items

1. **No staging domain, so nothing here is verifiable end to end.** `.planning/external-applications.md` item #2 is `not-started`. Railway carries `BASE_DOMAIN=staging.boutique-platform.invalid` (an RFC 2606 reserved TLD), so **every request to the staging API 404s at the tenant middleware today** and only `/health` answers — which is precisely why the deploy smoke check passes. Consequences to record rather than fight: no `*.{domain}` DNS means no `{slug}.{domain}` to open; E1's criterion at `e1-platform-foundation.md:18` and F2 Tasks 3 & 5 stay open; and the browser-preflight verification F8's Risk 2 deferred stays deferred (though **not** for F10's read path — a plain `<img src>` is a no-CORS subresource load and never consults `AllowedOrigins`). **F10 is fully buildable, unit/integration/CI-green and locally verifiable on `*.localtest.me`; it cannot be signed off in staging.** Owner: user. Trigger: now.
2. **No frontend deploy pipeline exists at all.** `ci.yml`'s `deploy-staging` runs `railway up --service api` and `--service worker` from `backend/`; the `frontend` job builds and stops. F10 ships build-only and E2's third success criterion closes on local + CI evidence. **Added requirement, owned here**: whatever eventually serves the SPA `index.html` must emit `X-Frame-Options: DENY` and `X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff` — that document, not the JSON API, is the clickjacking target for the manage session cookie, and F10's middleware cannot reach it. Owner: F2 follow-up. Trigger: domain purchased.
3. **The public catalog is uncacheable and serves unprocessed originals with no CDN.** `no-store` is mandatory because the bodies carry 900 s bearer URLs. **And the browser cache does not help**: `generate_presigned_url` stamps a fresh `X-Amz-Date`/`X-Amz-Signature` on every call, so the same S3 object yields a *different URL string* — a different cache key — on every page load. A bride who opens `/`, taps a dress and taps back re-downloads all 24 originals from `il-central-1` each time. (An earlier draft claimed `SIGNED_GET_TTL_SECONDS` "lets the browser cache within a session"; it does not, and quantizing the signing timestamp to make it true is not reachable through boto3's API without overriding the signer's clock — not worth it against the real fix.) A 24-card grid is up to 24 × 10 MiB. Mitigations actually in scope: the 10 MiB upload cap, `loading="lazy"` on every card image (the new `DressCard` attribute), the 24 default **and the 24 max**, and F8's in-product upload-guidance line. The real fix is thumbnails + a CDN, deferred to E10 and bound by media-origin isolation. **Additionally owned by the user, not deferred**: an AWS budget alarm and an S3 request-count alarm on the media bucket, since nothing in the application bounds egress. Owner: E10 (thumbnails) / user (alarms). Trigger: the first bucket cost review, or boutique #2.
4. **The read throttle is a brake, not a defence.** In-process and single-instance (so it resets on deploy and does not aggregate across replicas), keyed per tenant (so a scraper degrades the boutique it scrapes rather than being isolated from good visitors), and it does not bound S3 egress at all — a minted URL is redeemable by anyone, any number of times, for 900 s. Sized at 6000/60 s specifically so organic traffic cannot trip it; a 429 renders the retry state. Separately, tenant resolution itself is one un-throttled indexed DB lookup per request for any syntactically valid non-reserved slug (`ROADMAP.md:121`), which a crawled public surface makes materially worse. Owner: F21 (+ E5 #29's bounded negative-result cache). Trigger: the first observed scrape, or replica count > 1.
5. **Five design bets shipped unvalidated.** `test-results.md` records 0 of 8 participant sessions; the interview gate was waived by user directive 2026-07-25. Three of the five are F10's: whether a mixed price grid reads as *expensive* or as *hiding something*; whether "הוזמן" reads as "this place is picked over" (**critical**); and whether a `ContactPanel` appearing instead of a booking form reads as relief or as broken (**critical** — if it reads as a dead end, the CTA needs different copy until E3 ships). The fourth, the zero-dress identity moment, is the state the pilot actually launches in. Owner: pilot. Trigger: first ten real storefront visitors — post-ship validation items with named kill conditions, not vague concerns.
6. **`qa-checklist.md` §§6–11 carries ~15 storefront requirements absent from the three screen docs**, and the docs are 3 KB rev-2 sketches against `manage-catalog.md`'s 152 KB rev-4. Ruling recorded above (checklist co-binding, later wins; one item F9-discharged; one screen-doc item overruled as unbuildable). The risk is that a builder reads only the screen docs. Mitigation: the plan's per-task Gotchas must cite `qa-checklist.md` line items directly, and the dual review's brief must include a checklist walk. Owner: F10 build. Trigger: now.
7. **`essence` is empty on every existing boutique** until an owner fills it, so `BoutiqueHeader` renders three of its four parts on day one. The prop is optional and degrades cleanly; the checklist item is about the populated default. Pilot-onboarding consequences: `essence` and `instagram` belong on the boutique-onboarding script alongside photos, **and the script must state plainly that `phone`, `address`, `description` and `maps_url` appear on the public page** — the console helper line says it at the point of entry, the script says it before she types.

- **`price_agorot: null` rather than key omission.** F8's Note for F10 says the storefront "must omit the field server-side" when `price_visible` is false. F10 sends the key with a `null` value instead, and drops `price_visible` entirely. Reason: the rationale is about the *value* — "a hidden price that ships to the browser and is merely not rendered is not hidden" — and `null` discharges it completely; a Pydantic model cannot conditionally omit a declared field without `exclude_unset` gymnastics that would also drop other legitimately-null fields; and collapsing hidden-price and no-price to one wire value means a client cannot distinguish them, which is *stronger* than key omission. **Flag for the reviewer**: a deliberate reading of a prior spec's binding language, not an oversight.
- **`packages/ui` takes two additive optional props.** Reason: `DressCard` and `Gallery` own their `<img>` elements, so `onError` and `loading` are unobservable from the app, and F8's line 203 plus Risks 3 both require them. The alternative was overriding a binding F8 note. Bounded to two optional props with no visual change and no F9 test touched, so the passed gate is not reopened.
- **`STOREFRONT_LIST_MAX_LIMIT = 24`, not 100.** Reason: the UI never asks for more, and each unit is one more 900 s bearer URL per unauthenticated call, redeemable by anyone. Symmetry with the authenticated manage list is not an argument for a public endpoint.
- **The load-more ships; the filter does not.** They were one line in `insights.md` and are two different things: the filter is an enhancement, the load-more is the only way to reach dress 25 of ~60.
- **A hand-rolled ~80-line router over `react-router`.** Reason: no router exists in the monorepo, four static routes and one dynamic segment, and `DressCard` already emits a raw `<a href>` that click delegation intercepts without a prop change. It carries real obligations the naive version misses — the exclusion list (hash, `target`, `download`, `rel=external`, non-http protocols) and focus + scroll management on navigate — and those are written into the spec rather than discovered. If the build finds delegation fighting the platform, take `react-router` and say so — **this is the decision most likely to be wrong**.
- **`/about` uses `Button`, not `BookingCTA`.** `BookingCTA`'s base class is `fixed … bottom-0` with only `md:static`, and `cn` is a naive joiner with no tailwind-merge, so the caller cannot override it. §7 requires no bottom bar on `/about` at any width.
- **`errorMessage()` maps codes to i18n keys instead of rendering the server string.** Manage does the latter and shows English in a Hebrew console; that is a pre-existing manage issue and stays out of scope, but a public Hebrew-only page cannot repeat it (§10).
- **`TenantContext.name` gets a default.** Reason: nine hand-built contexts across five test files, one production construction site. A required field turns a one-line change into a five-file red push for zero safety.
- **`StorefrontThrottledError` gets its own handler** rather than reparenting the three existing throttle errors onto a shared base. Reason: the consolidation is right and behaviour-neutral, but it is cross-module churn in a feature PR; three lines here, the cleanup in F21.
- **Docs and the OpenAPI schema go dark outside dev, in this PR.** Reason: F10 is what puts the origin in front of crawlers, and `/openapi.json` publishes the exact field names the allowlist exists to hide. The `EXEMPT_PATHS` comment's justification — feeding api-client generation — is void anyway now that F10 declines the generated client.
- **`@boutique/api-client` stays empty; the generated wrapper re-homes to E3 #14.**
- **`/about` stays a separate route**, not a section of `/`. Per-route title (WCAG 2.4.2), the footer is the specified entry, and a 640px editorial column does not belong inside a 1200px grid page.
- **`essence` and `instagram` added to F7's profile by F10**, together with the public-visibility helper line under the profile section. `whatsapp` and `wazeUrl` are derived, not stored.
- **`X-Frame-Options: DENY` platform-wide**, with the honest rationale: API defense-in-depth. The clickjacking fix for the console is an HTML-serving-layer requirement recorded in Risks 2.
- **The dress-detail screen doc's "full-bleed tap opens the image native-size" is overruled**, because F8 pins `attachment` disposition on every signed GET and the affordance would produce a file download.
- **Card `alt` stays the dress name; the gallery main image takes the position string.** §6/§8 bind the card and F9 shipped it; the gallery's eight-identical-strings problem is F10's to choose and is fixed here.
