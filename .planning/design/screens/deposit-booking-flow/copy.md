## 0. The rules this deck is written under — hard constraints, not preferences

1. **Zero exclamation marks** (pre-decided #5). The product ships none; `he.ts` in both apps is mechanically checkable and `__tests__/i18n.test.ts` already reads for it. One here would be the single string that breaks the register — and on a money screen it would also read as a sales voice the product does not have.
2. **Never claim, promise or hedge that a message was sent, in any tense.** Not trivially satisfied this time: F19 **does** send an SMS (**MD2**), and the owner-facing marker that fires on the same branch sits one line away from *"we let her know"*, which is the exact sentence a well-meaning editor adds. `_deliver` swallows both send errors, so the platform has **no evidence** anything was delivered — the shipped `booking.deliveryNotice` says so out loud. No string in §4 mentions the SMS.
3. **The deposit is «מקדמה». Never «פיקדון».** The product has said «מקדמה» since F7 — `profile.depositsEnabled` («גביית מקדמות מופעלת»), `booking.depositByPhone` («לסגור יחד גם את המקדמה»), `booking.forfeitSuffix` («מהמקדמה.»), and the manage console's own `TermsSection` («חילוט 50% מהמקדמה»). **The spec's MD2 and MD3 drafts both use «פיקדון»** (`spec:86`, `spec:115`); this deck changes the word and not the meaning, for F57's rule 8 reason — a second spelling of one money noun in one product is a defect the day somebody edits one of them.
4. **The boutique is «הבוטיק». Never «הסלון».** Same rule, same evidence: `manage.invalidHint` («אפשר להתקשר לבוטיק»), `booking.cancelledByOwner` («הבוטיק»), `about.heading` («על הבוטיק»). **MD3's drafted interim says «של הסלון»** (`spec:115`); this deck corrects it.
5. **No string states a refund or forfeit outcome, in either direction.** **D16** computes the number and **writes no `refund_due` / `refunded` / `forfeited` row at all**; F29 is the earliest anything can perform one. The only two sentences in the product that will ever say what she gets back are **MD3's parked pair**, and they are the user's. Every string here that touches a cancelled-and-paid booking says what the boutique will *do* (hold it, call her), never what she is *entitled to* — the line MD2 draws at `spec:82` and the reason MD2 is not parked and MD3 is.
6. **No string names a number, a percentage, a window or a currency.** Money renders **only** through the shipped `<Price>` component — `agorot / 100` → `Intl.NumberFormat("he-IL")` → `<bdi dir="ltr">{formatted} ₪</bdi>` (`packages/ui/src/components/Price.tsx:16-31`). `Frontend/scripts/qa-greps.sh:37` fails the build on a `₪` glyph anywhere under `apps/storefront/src`, and **R19** forbids interpolating a value i18next cannot bidi-isolate. Every amount in this deck is therefore **split out of its sentence** into a bare label beside `<Price>` — the `booking.confirmWhen` / `confirmWhat` / `confirmDress` shape, which is R19's strongest form because it has no seam at all.
7. **No string names or implies a duration, an interval or a deadline.** Inherited from F34's rule 9 and F57's rule 5, and it bites harder here: the hold is `deposit_hold_seconds` (default 900, **one env var, reversible in a deploy** — `spec:359`), the sweeper's worst case is a poll interval on top of it (Risk 6), and the provider's retry budget against a 15-minute hold is **explicitly unknowable until F18** (`spec:688`). Any string naming «15 דקות» is false the first time that variable moves. States **what is unknown**, or names the **event**, never the clock.
8. **No string names the payment provider, and none may be added.** *"F19 names no provider anywhere and builds on `PaymentGateway` / `PaymentService` only"* (`spec:690`). `booking.depositNotTaken` says «שירות התשלומים», which names a function and not a company, and is the only string that mentions one at all.
9. **No string explains a decline.** The webhook carries `paid=false` and nothing the product may repeat to her — the reason belongs to her bank and to the provider. «התשלום לא הושלם» is the whole of what we know. Never «נדחה», never «הכרטיס נדחה», never «אין כיסוי».
10. **Every value is a real string.** No `…`-as-placeholder, no Lorem, nothing to be filled in later. **The one exception is the parked row** and it is marked INTERIM rather than blank, because a blank there is precisely the shipped-and-false sentence it replaces.
11. **The `ar` value is the approved Hebrew, standing in untranslated** (Interview Q3 / pre-decided #47), and it is **never an empty string**: i18next's `returnEmptyString` default renders `""` rather than falling back, so a premature switch would blank the page instead of showing Hebrew (`storefront/src/i18n/ar.ts:14-19`, `manage/src/i18n/ar.ts:14-20`). `lng` stays `"he"`, `fallbackLng` stays `"he"`, no switcher ships. **This deck carries no `ar` column** — a divergence from `shift-board/copy.md`'s five-column shape, taken deliberately: the value is the `he` cell verbatim for all 25 rows, and 25 duplicated cells is 25 places for a typo to enter. §6 states the mechanical rule once and §7 measures it.

### 3.1 The parked item — what is missing, who owns it, and what it costs to land

**What ships now is genuinely neutral, and that is the whole design of it.** «המקדמה מטופלת בהתאם למדיניות הביטולים של הבוטיק.» names **no number, no window, no percentage and no direction**. It is true under every possible answer to the parked question, it promises nothing either way, and the policy she accepted is already on the same screen two lines above it — `manage.cancelPolicyLead` + the isolated `refundable_until_hours_before` + `manage.cancelPolicySuffix`, all shipped and all APPROVED. She is therefore not left without the window; she is left without a sentence telling her what the money does on either side of it.

**What is missing is two approved Hebrew sentences, and they are the user's to supply** — one for **inside** the refund window (she cancels, the deposit comes back, in whole or in part) and one for **outside** it (the boutique keeps some or all of it). An engineer may decide a hold length, a status value or a compensating transaction; an engineer may **not** invent consumer-facing copy that tells a bride who paid ₪500 whether that ₪500 comes back. That is a representation the boutique is held to, not a description of a mechanism — the test MD2's SMS passes (it promises an *action*) and this one does not (it promises *money*). It is also, uniquely in this feature, a **correction to a sentence real customers have already read**.

**Landing them is a string change and nothing structural.** The in/out-of-window branch **already ships as structure** — pre-decided #4 put it there at F16 and had both sides render the same sentence until E4 (`manage-booking/copy.md:22`). So the swap is: this one key becomes two, both values go into `he.ts` and `ar.ts` in both files, and the component branches on **D16**'s already-computed number (`terms_versions.refundable_until_hours_before` and `forfeit_percent` against the booking's snapshotted `terms_version_accepted` and `starts_at` — every input is on the row today). **No schema, no API, no migration, no logic, and nothing downstream.** F19 must not wait for it, and MD3's hard constraint is satisfied the moment this interim renders.

## 5. The SMS — `PAYMENT_RECEIVED_NO_SLOT` (**MD2**)

**The only path in the whole flow where a customer's money moves and nothing tells her**, until this body exists. Sent from **D5 step 4** — the branch where a late webhook was honoured, the seat was gone, and the booking stayed `cancelled` with the payment `paid`. Race rows **#5** and **#15**.

This is **not an i18n key.** It lands in `Backend/app/booking/comms_templates.py` beside the four shipped bodies, as a pure function with no I/O, and it is delivered through `BookingCommsService._deliver`, which is built.

> **{boutique}: התשלום שלך התקבל, אך המועד שבחרת כבר נתפס. המקדמה שמורה עבורך ונחזור אליך בהקדם. לשאלות ולתיאום מחדש: {phone}**
>
> *{boutique}: your payment was received, but the time you chose is already taken. The deposit is held for you and we will get back to you shortly. For questions and to arrange a new time: {phone}*

**Status: PROPOSED.** The counsel gate `comms_templates.py:1-9` records for the four shipped bodies applies to this one identically — *"counsel signs the bodies off before a real provider goes live"*, a **pre-provider gate and not a pre-merge one**. F21 UAT is where the owner reads it, and per MD2 that review **does not gate the merge** and is **not a second parked item**.

**Segment cost.** Hebrew is UCS-2 on the wire: **70 characters in a single message, 67 once concatenated** (6 of the 70 go to the UDH), and "characters" means UTF-16 code units. The body is **82 units** before interpolation. With the shipped `BOUTIQUE_NAME_MAX_CHARS = 25` truncation and a 15-character phone budget the worst case is 144 units → **3 segments**; a typical 12-character boutique name and an 11-digit mobile is 127 → **2 segments**. So: **2 segments in practice, 3 at the documented budget ceiling**, and it takes a `PAYMENT_RECEIVED_NO_SLOT_MAX_SEGMENTS = 3` constant pinned by a test, exactly like the four bodies beside it. An invoice is never how a fourth segment should be discovered.

**Four things this body deliberately does not do, each of which is the obvious edit.**

1. **It promises no refund**, and names no sum, window or percentage. It states what the boutique will *do* — hold the deposit, call her — which is the line MD2 draws to stay out of MD3's parked territory (`spec:82`). **D16** writes no `refund_due` row at all, so a refund promise here would be a representation with no mechanism behind it anywhere in the product until F29.
2. **It names no new time, and does not promise one.** One body has to be true in **two** races. Row #5 is the ordinary case where a new time is exactly right. Row **#15** is the bride who rebooked the same instant herself: her deposit is stranded on a cancelled row while her **live booking stands**, so «נקבע לך מועד חדש» would be wrong copy on the hardest case to explain. «נחזור אליך» is true in both, and the specific remedy is the owner's to say on the phone.
3. **It carries no date and no manage link.** The date is the trap: on row #15 naming the taken date would read as though her live booking had been lost, on the one message where she is already anxious. And the booking this row belongs to is `cancelled` — a manage link points at a dead appointment, and D8 has already blanked the checkout URL.
4. **It claims nothing was already done.** «התקבל» is a fact about money the webhook verified; «שמורה» is what MD1 makes true; «נחזור אליך» is a promise the boutique keeps, and Risk 4 names it as a **new and narrower failure mode** — a promise made by the product and kept by a person. §4's `booking.paymentActionNeeded` is the whole of what makes that person act.

⚠ **The phone clause is this deck's one divergence from MD2's drafted body** (`spec:86`), and it is a reuse rather than an invention: `owner_cancel_sms_body` already ends *"לשאלות ולתיאום מחדש: {boutique_phone}"* and already **drops the clause entirely** when the boutique published no phone, because *"«לשאלות:» followed by nothing is worse than a shorter sentence"*. Same clause, same null rule, and it costs no extra segment in the typical case. It gives her agency on a screen-less surface where she is otherwise waiting for a call she cannot chase. **Strike it and the spec's exact drafted body is the fallback**, one line shorter and still correct.

**The kind, and where it lands.** `MessageKind.PAYMENT_RECEIVED_NO_SLOT = "payment_received_no_slot"` — lowercase, matching every shipped member (`otp`, `confirmation`, `owner_cancel`, `owner_reschedule`), appended to `Backend/app/models/constants.py:32-38`. ⚠ Two corrections for the builder: the enum class is **`MessageKind`**, not `MessageLogKind` — `message_log.kind` is the *column*. And **`constants.py` is a live merge surface**: F57's branch is widening `StaffRole` in the same file right now, so this is an **append to a different enum** — rebase before every push, never touch F57's role values. The `message_log.kind` CHECK widening is migration **statement 4**, and its number is **not 0014**: F19 builds against **0015 / down_revision 0014** so its own branch is self-coherent, and renumbers to **0017 / down_revision 0016** at rebase, after F57 (0015) and F33 (0016) merge. MD2's declined alternative (c) — reusing `owner_reschedule` to dodge the CHECK — stays declined: `kind` is the field a compliance read and an SMS-cost audit both group on.

## 6. The `ar` rule — stated once, because it is the same rule 25 times

Every one of the 25 keys gets an `ar` entry whose value is **the `he` cell of its row, verbatim**, in `Frontend/apps/storefront/src/i18n/ar.ts` and `Frontend/apps/manage/src/i18n/ar.ts`. Not a translation, not a transliteration, and **never an empty string** — i18next's `returnEmptyString` default renders `""` rather than falling back, so a premature switch would blank the page instead of showing Hebrew. The SHIPPED rows in §2, §3 and §4 are **not** re-declared: `manage.cancelConsequenceFree` and `booking.rescheduleCta` already carry their `ar` entries, and `booking.cancelledNoActions` does too.

**The enforcement is not symmetric, and the deck says so rather than repeating a claim it did not check.**

- **Manage console: mechanical, and it needs no test edit.** `manage/src/__tests__/i18n.test.ts:262-265` asserts every key in the feature's prefix blocks exists in `ar.translation`, and `:252-259` asserts no `ar` value is `""`. All nine of F19's console keys begin `booking.`, so `HE_F15` collects them automatically and a missing `ar` key **red-fails**.
- **Storefront: there is no parity guard at all.** `storefront/src/__tests__/i18n-keys.test.ts` never imports `ar` — it scans `src/` for dotted literals and asserts each resolves to non-empty Hebrew in `he` only. `ar.ts` is imported by `i18n/index.ts` and by nothing else. So **a missing storefront `ar` key is silent today**, and stays silent right up until a switcher ships. The brief for this deck stated both apps enforce it; one does. **F19 should copy the manage guard's four lines into the storefront suite** — it is a `Object.keys(he.translation.booking)`-shaped filter over the 16 storefront keys, it costs one `it()`, and it is the only thing that makes this section's rule true rather than aspirational.
