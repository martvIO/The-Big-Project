## 5. Build discipline

**Commit boundaries** — one per task, conventional and scoped: `feat(platform): platform_invites migration` (A1) · `feat(platform): invite model and repository, code hashed at rest` (A2) · `refactor(platform): extract _create_tenant at parity` (B1) · `feat(platform): create, list and revoke invites` (B2) · `feat(platform): single-use invite redemption in one transaction` (B3) · `feat(platform): operator invite endpoints` (C1) · `feat(platform): anonymous join preview and redeem routes` (C2) · `test(security): register the five platform routes with every walker` (C3) · `feat(platform-app): serve and route /platform/join` (D1) · `feat(platform-app): invite and join copy, he and ar` (D2) · `feat(platform-app): join panel` (D3) · `feat(platform-app): invites section and one-time link panel` (D4) · `test(e2e): invite and join journeys with axe` (E1).

**Migration renumber AND re-parent protocol.** The file is numbered **observed-head+1 in this worktree at cut time**. Immediately before the pre-push rebase, run `alembic heads` against rebased main and fix **both** halves in one `fix(platform):` commit:
1. **Renumber** — filename and the `revision` string. A duplicate `revision` breaks `alembic upgrade` outright and reds every db test.
2. **Re-parent** — `down_revision` must name the **rebased head**, not the head observed at cut time. Two revisions naming the same parent **branches the history**: `alembic heads` returns two, `upgrade head` refuses, `test_exactly_one_migration_head` reds, and **every db test goes red** behind it.
