### CHECK constraints — exactly two, both NAMED, both their own statement

Named and separate is the 0011 shape (`staff_users_role_check`, `0011:27-30`) and is what makes a later widening a one-line edit rather than a guess at a Postgres-generated name. `test_migrations.py:107-134` already has the drop/re-add-by-name helper pattern this borrows.

```sql
ALTER TABLE customers ADD CONSTRAINT customers_marketing_consent_source_check
  CHECK (marketing_consent_source IN ('booking_form'));

ALTER TABLE customers ADD CONSTRAINT customers_marketing_withdraw_check
  CHECK (marketing_consent_withdrawn_at IS NULL OR marketing_consent_at IS NOT NULL);
```

**Declined, and on the record**: a third CHECK `(marketing_consent_at IS NULL OR marketing_consent_source IS NOT NULL)`. ## 6. Build discipline

**Rebase protocol (migration).** Build the migration as `0024` and keep it the **last commit on the branch**. Immediately before the pre-push rebase, re-run `alembic heads` against rebased `main`; if a sibling feature took 0024, `git commit --amend` the one file. `.memory/parallel-alembic-numbering` records that a reserved-but-wrong number breaks alembic outright and reds **every** db test, so this check is not skippable.

**Pathspec.** Git tracks `backend/`/`frontend/` **lowercase** though the dirs are `Backend/`/`Frontend/`. `git add Backend/…` **silently skips modified tracked files**. Lowercase every pathspec and verify with `git show --stat` before pushing (`.memory/git-add-uppercase-pathspec-trap`).

**Merge-conflict hygiene.** A conflict-broken test file reads as one `Tests no tests` line, not N failures (`.memory/silently-unexecuted-test-files`). After any conflict resolution, parse every touched test file's collection count.

**Parallel sessions share this worktree.** Files and commits can change mid-task; a clean `git status` does not mean idle. Sweep with `find … -mmin` before touching a sibling area.

## 7. Risks the plan discovered that the spec did not

**R-A — the walk-in consent promotion is now an orphan.** `app/models/queue_ticket.py` and `app/queue/service.py:120-124` both name **F20** as the owner of promoting a consenting walk-in ticket into `customers`. DR-10 declines it, with reasons. The result is a shipped comment pointing at a feature that has decided not to do the thing. **Mitigation**: the plan's DR-10 entry is the record, and the compliance record's §1 must state plainly that a walk-in marketing opt-in is a *submission record* on `queue_tickets`, not a *consent record* on `customers`, and that no send path consults it. **Owner: user/team. Trigger: F46 — the first marketing send is the first thing that needs to know which store to read, and it must not read both.**

**R-B — the 7-year `bookings` PURGE orphans `payments` rows that cannot be deleted.** `payments.booking_id` is a no-FK pointer and `0012:147-148` revoked `DELETE` on `payments` from `app_user` deliberately (*"a hard DELETE of a payment row destroys financial evidence"*). So policy 5 leaves a payment row pointing at a booking that no longer exists, permanently, with no mechanism able to remove it. This is not new data loss — it is a **dangling financial record** and it is the exact shape D8 used to argue customers must be scrubbed rather than purged. **Reading taken**: accepted for F20 (a payment row carries no name, no phone and no free text — `provider_session_id`, amounts, timestamps), recorded rather than engineered around. **Upgrade path**: if the 7-year purge is ever actually reached, either scrub `payments.provider_*` at the same clock or leave the bookings row as a SCRUB too. **Owner: team. Trigger: F21's audit, or the first tenant approaching 7 years — neither is near.**

**R-C — CLOSED at review.** F33's live `checkin.notice` tells every walk-in «הפרטים … נמחקים כמה ימים לאחר הביקור», and nothing deletes them today — an express representation a data subject can rely on, which is worse than the silence F20 was chartered to fix. The plan offered (a) accept it until F21, or (b) soften the sentence in D1's replacement copy. **(b) was taken and the copy is written**: the replacement says «את הפרטים אנחנו שומרות רק כל עוד הם דרושים לניהול התור», which is true with `retention_enabled=False` *and* after F21 flips it. F20 no longer merges carrying a false public representation. The residual is only that the notice states a purpose limitation rather than a period — recorded, not open.

**R-D — `gateway_superseded_credential_retention_days = 90` is declared in `Settings` and read by nothing.** `config.py:157`; `grep` over `Backend/app/` finds no reader. F17 declared a retention period and never wired it. It is out of F20's scope (a superseded credential is not a data subject's personal data), and F20 must **not** absorb it — the table has no `DELETE` grant, so a policy naming it would fail at the grant, which is exactly what DR-8's test now catches. Recorded so the next reader of the retention registry does not assume it is covered. **Owner: team. Trigger: F21's audit.**

**R-E — CORRECTED at review: the guard this risk relied on does not exist, and the real exposure is larger.** The first draft said `i18n-keys.test.ts:162-171` *"asserts `he`/`ar` byte-equality across a parity list"* covering the copy swap. It does not. That block (`:152-173`) is scoped by an explicit decision to exactly `["checkin.guideTrigger", "checkin.guideHint"]`, and its own comment says why: *"Deliberately scoped to these two keys (DL21) … **F20's counsel swap makes `checkin.notice` a two-file edit ON PURPOSE**, and a blanket parity guard would have to be relaxed the day Arabic is actually translated."*

**R-H — a walk-in with no `customers` row is invisible to the subject-request panel, and Strings 6/1 state rights the console cannot serve her.** DR-10 declines the queue-ticket→customer promotion; a customer row is only ever created inside `create_booking`; `subject-export` resolves through `CustomersRepository.by_phone` (`customers.py:49-57`, exact equality). ## 9. Review findings raised and REJECTED

Recorded because a silently dropped finding is indistinguishable from an oversight to the next reader. The spec uses this convention; so does `copy.md`.

1. **"Add `AND marketing_opt_in_at IS NULL` to the `queue_tickets` retention predicate and give opted-in tickets their own longer or unbounded class"** — offered as the preferred fix for the DR-11 blocker, and described as *"the smallest diff and the only one that does not narrow what she was already told"*.

   **Rejected on the second half of that claim; the blocker itself is applied in full.** It is a small diff, but what it preserves is not a subject protection — it is a promise, made in the **boutique's** commercial favour, to retain her name and phone indefinitely. Taken literally it means an opted-in walk-in's contact detail is held with **no clock at all**, in a store that R-A guarantees no send path will read and that the spec's Out-of-scope guarantees has no consumer in v1 (`MessageKind` gains no member). That is "nothing is ever deleted" re-created inside the feature whose Problem statement opens by naming it. It would also cost a **seventh** `Settings` key with its own floor and a **second** policy for a table with one reader — the exact shape D8 declined when it refused a generic declarative policy and D9 declined when it refused per-tenant retention.

   What she is entitled to is the **ability to revoke**, and no design on the table served that: the withdraw route could not reach a queue ticket at all. The resolution taken builds the revocation half (`phone` arm, A9/C5) and drops the retention half, which is protective rather than merely faithful to a sentence that should not have been written. Both halves are pinned by test — `test_queue_db.py` for the arm, `test_retention_db.py` for "an opted-in ticket is scrubbed like any other" — so neither can be quietly reverted.

   The two remaining options were also weighed. Reversing DR-10 (promote the walk-in into `customers`) would launder an **unverified** submission into Spam-Law evidence and degrade every row in `marketing_consent_at`, which is the distinction `queue/service.py:120-124` exists to preserve. Cutting Strings 6 and 7 from the copy approval is rejected separately and on the record in `copy.md`.
